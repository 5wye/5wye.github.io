⚠️ Avoid These

  • • Chrome while gaming
  • • Auto-updates during play
  • • Recording/streaming
  • • High graphics settings
  • • AAA games (2020+)

Reality Check: Low-end hardware has limits. These tweaks help, but won't turn a potato into a gaming beast. Expect 30-60 FPS on low settings in most games. Some AAA titles won't run at all.
